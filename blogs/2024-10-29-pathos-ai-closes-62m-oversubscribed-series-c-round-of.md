CHICAGO, Oct. 29, 2024 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) — Pathos AI, (www.pathos.com), a clinical-stage biotechnology company focused on re-engineering drug development through artificial intelligence, today announced the closing of an oversubscribed $62M Series C funding round. The Series C financing round was led by New Enterprise Associates (NEA) with participation from Revolution Growth and other existing insiders.
